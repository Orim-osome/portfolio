DriveX — Car Rental Marketplace

DriveX is a marketplace concept designed to connect independent vehicle providers with people looking to rent a car. It is being built as an ASP.NET Core application backed by PostgreSQL.

The application has separate experiences for administrators, providers and renters. Current functionality includes account registration and login, vehicle listings, booking workflows, payment verification and email verification.

Status: Still in development; not publicly deployed. No public demo is available yet.
